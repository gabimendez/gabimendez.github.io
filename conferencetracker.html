01 ABOUT
THE
RESOURCE

The Conference Tracker is a resource designed to support faculty, administrators, and staff by providing a quick overview of selected conferences from around the world.

It brings together key information such as conference modality, location, important dates, and potential fees, making it easier to explore opportunities at a glance.

The tracker focuses on conferences related to International Education, Global Learning, Collaborative Online International Learning (COIL), Virtual Exchange, Teaching and Learning, and other closely connected areas.

These events play an important role in fostering collaboration, innovation, and the internationalization of education.

WHY IT MATTERS

Conferences can be valuable spaces for discovering ideas, building partnerships, sharing practice, and staying connected with communities working across borders.
