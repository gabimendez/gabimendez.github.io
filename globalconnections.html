01 ABOUT
THE
CONNECTIONS

Global Connections is a growing record of the institutions represented by faculty and educators I've had the opportunity to work with through professional development, COIL, virtual exchange, and collaborative learning.

It reflects the people behind the partnerships and learning experiences that have shaped my work over the years — across countries, regions, disciplines, languages, and educational contexts.

Rather than simply counting institutions or countries, this collection represents the relationships, conversations, and shared learning experiences that make global learning meaningful.

WHY IT MATTERS

Global learning grows through relationships. Every institution represents educators, ideas, perspectives, and opportunities to learn with and from one another.
