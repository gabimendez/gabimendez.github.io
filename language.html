LANGUAGE
ACKNOWLEDGEMENT.

A simple but meaningful way to recognize linguistic diversity in COIL and Virtual Exchange activities.

01 WHY
LANGUAGE
MATTERS

Language is more than a communication tool. It shapes how we express ourselves, participate, connect with others, and make meaning.

In COIL and Virtual Exchange, participants may be communicating in a language that is not their first or most comfortable language.

A simple acknowledgement can help create a more inclusive environment by recognizing the effort involved in communicating across languages.

A SMALL GESTURE.

Acknowledging linguistic diversity can open the door to greater participation, empathy, and connection across cultures.

02 · HOW TO USE IT

KEEP IT
SIMPLE.

01

ADAPT

Adapt the acknowledgement to your context, activity, and audience.

02

OPEN

Use it at the beginning of a session, event, or collaborative activity.

03

INCLUDE

Include at least two languages whenever possible and appropriate for your context.

03
SAMPLE · ENGLISH

SPEAK
FROM THE
HEART.

“Before we start our activities, we want to share that although the language used for this session will be English, we acknowledge and promote linguistic diversity. We recognize the effort involved in communicating in a foreign language and encourage all participants to speak from the heart and include expressions from their mother tongue.”
04
EJEMPLO · ESPAÑOL

HABLAR
DESDE EL
CORAZÓN.

“Antes de iniciar nuestras actividades, queremos compartir con ustedes que aunque el idioma utilizado para esta sesión será el inglés, reconocemos y promovemos la diversidad lingüística. Reconocemos el esfuerzo que implica comunicarse en un idioma extranjero y nos gustaría animar a todos los participantes a hablar desde el corazón y sentirse libres para incluir palabras y expresiones de su lengua materna.”
