MANUAL FOR
VIRTUAL INTERACTION.

A collaborative resource supporting inclusive, democratic, and highly collaborative virtual spaces for international projects and partnerships.

01 MY
CONTRIBUTION

The Manual for Virtual Interaction was developed through a collaborative effort led by the Mestenhauser Institute for International Collaboration, with contributions from the YOUROPE team.

I contributed my expertise in virtual collaboration, learning design, online interaction, and international education to the development of the manual.

The resource was designed to support collaboration across institutions and help create virtual spaces where every voice can be heard.

COLLABORATION BY DESIGN.

Meaningful virtual collaboration doesn't happen simply because we have the technology. It requires intentional design, inclusive practices, and attention to how people participate and connect.

03 · THE BIG IDEA

EVERY
VOICE
MATTERS.

Virtual spaces can either reproduce existing barriers or create new possibilities for participation and collaboration.

This manual brings together pedagogical and technical perspectives to help international project teams create spaces that are intentional, inclusive, and collaborative.
